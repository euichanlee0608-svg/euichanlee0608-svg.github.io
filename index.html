Projects

Broadband acoustic energy harvesting, where the contribution is robustness rather than peak power (power variation CoV 21% vs 29% across 11 measured noise environments; advantage grows with resonator quality factor). A Human-in-the-Loop, risk-aware bidding system for a virtual power plant, built with a team at a Korea University × Upstage hackathon (1st place, 23 teams), where I led the system architecture, objective function, and verification/feedback loop. A team-capstone autonomous pole-climbing and coconut-harvesting robot, where I led the control software and power electronics. A replay validation of sequential Bayesian optimization against four published experimental studies, with lookahead sealed and a random-order baseline: five wins, two draws, no losses, and adoption gated on measurement discriminability and design-space size. Asymmetric-geometry rotor fault tolerance. A short-range air-defense interception simulator with a playable in-browser demo, where four optimization methods are compared under proportional-navigation guidance.

Live web apps

Living Paper, a meeting assistant whose MVP is live: it marks the moment you pretended to understand and explains it afterwards from your own lab's documents, with the cloud relaying ciphertext only. Acoustic Harvester, an interactive version of the harvesting study. Divert-Sim, a playable interception simulator. VPP Trade-Guard, a case study of the hackathon system. Engineering Brain, which poses engineering questions and grades the answers. News Literacy Lab, which breaks a news article down by stakeholder. Emotion Quadrant, a Korean-language tool for naming a feeling precisely. 357 Stick Breaker, a misère Nim variant played against a solver.

Systems and engineering judgment

Safeguards designed so no tool grades itself: research change records come from the git diff rather than a model's narration, regression gates replay real messages through real handlers instead of checking liveness, remote automation passes approval steps with per-thread heartbeats and terminal fallback, and a physics-equation catalogue is graded by evidence (reference, dimensional, numeric) behind a 95% CI gate. Low-quality experiments are dropped honestly; an engineering-reasoning dataset now feeds a planned campus poster quantifying personalized in-context learning for engineering Q&A.
